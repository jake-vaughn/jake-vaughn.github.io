## Who am I?

Hello!
My name is `Jake Vaughn`, and I earned a B.S. in Computer Engineering from Iowa State University. I currently work as a Software Engineer at Vermeer Corporation, where I contribute to the BorePlan project.

## Professional Experience

I have been with Vermeer Corporation since January 2023, where I’ve been developing BorePlan, a cross-platform mobile application used to plan, execute, and document horizontal directional drilling (HDD) projects. In this role, I’ve architected and implemented bore path generation algorithms that enable precise, rod-by-rod HDD planning, along with utility collision detection to improve safety and accuracy.
